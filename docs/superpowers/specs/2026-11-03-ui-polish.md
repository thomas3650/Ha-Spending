## 1. The page in general

### 1.1 Loading is faster, and the placeholder holds the layout (#82)

Today a change of Periode or of Gruppér pr. fetches new data (the fetched span depends on both, since it reaches one
unit, or 12, back for the comparisons). While it loads, every card turns into a grey placeholder. A change of accounts,
the level or a card does not fetch.

Claude measured `/api/data` on the real add-on on 2026-11-03, uncached (only times and counts were read):

| Span | Postings | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A month with no postings | 0 | 1.4 s |
| A month | about 100 | 1.5 s |
| A year | about 1,100 | 2.2 s |
| 13 months | about 1,400 | 2.5 s |
| All | about 3,400 | 6.0 s |

A cached answer took 0.01–0.04 s. So about 1.4 s of every load goes to what does not depend on the span (the rules,
the categories, the accounts, the earliest date, the subscriptions), and about 0.7 s to each page of 500 postings,
since the pages come one after another (technical spec §3.3).

Thomas, 2026-11-03: make the server faster and keep placeholders; no two-step load for now, measure again after.

**The server (changes technical spec §3.3):**

- The parts that do not depend on the span are read once and kept 5 minutes, as the answers are: the main-group rules,
  the categories, the asset accounts, the earliest posting date and the subscriptions. They are kept per day (`today`),
  so a new day reads them again. A new span then reads only its postings.
- The postings of a span are read with the first page alone, then the remaining pages at most 4 at a time, instead of
  one after another. Every list read through `getAllPages` works this way. The order of the result is the pages' order,
  as today.
- The 30 s timeout and the error mapping are unchanged. A failed read of the kept parts is not kept.
- The kept parts are read under their own signal, the 30 s timeout only, not under a span's request. Two spans asked
  at once share one read of them, and a failure in one span's postings does not abort it. A failure in the kept parts
  fails every span waiting on them.
- Tests (server, against the fake Firefly III):
  - two spans read in a row ask Firefly III for the rules, the categories, the accounts and the bills once; a new day
    asks again;
  - on a span of more than 5 pages, the fake sees more than one page request at a time and never more than 4, and the
    answer holds every posting in the pages' order;
  - after a failed read of the rules (or the bills), the next call asks Firefly III for them again;
  - two spans asked at once, one whose postings fail: the other answers with its data.

**The page:**

- While a load runs, the placeholder is shown as today, but its key-number row has as many boxes as the last answer
  showed (3 before any answer), laid out with the real row's columns (the same column count, up to 5, as the real row
  uses), so the row does not jump when the data comes.
- Each placeholder box is a list item of a list inside the busy group named `da.loading`, so the boxes can be counted
  by role.
- Tests: after a change of Periode on a page that showed 5 key-number boxes, the busy group's list has 5 items; on the
  first load it has 3.
- After the change, Claude measures the same spans again on the real add-on (reading only) and writes the times in the
  PR.

### 1.2 The right card's head row holds still (#85)

Today the head row is the title, then the chips (category, chosen unit), then the view switch and the full-screen
button, right-aligned; they drop to a second row when the title and the chips do not fit. With a category chosen, the
title names it ("El pr. måned"), as do the trail and the chip. "Forskel" appears in the middle of the switch when a
unit is chosen.

- With a category chosen, the bars', Posteringer's, Faste udgifter's and Butikker's titles name the category's main
  group, as with the main group alone ("Bolig pr. måned", not "El pr. måned"). The trail and the chip still name the
  category. Forskel keeps its title, which names the chosen unit: that is what the card compares.
- The title keeps to one line; a title too long for its room ends with "…" and has its full text as `title`.
- The chips go on their own line under the head row, inside the card, left-aligned. With no chip, the line is not
  there.
- The view switch's order is Søjler, Posteringer, Faste udgifter, Butikker, Forskel. Without a chosen unit, the
  Forskel button's place is still kept, empty and outside the switch's border (`visibility: hidden` and
  `aria-hidden="true"`, so it is out of the tab order and the accessibility tree). The bordered group then ends after
  Butikker, and with a unit chosen it grows into the kept place. So the tools' width is the same with and without a
  unit, and no button moves.
- With the example data at 1440 and 1728 px, normal and full screen, the head row stays on one line in every state: no
  chip, a category chosen, a unit chosen, both (the Chrome check).
- Tests: the switch's buttons in order by role and name, with and without a chosen unit (without one, no Forskel
  button by role); with a category chosen the heading's text is the main group's title and does not contain the
  category's name; the category chip is still there and removes the category.
- Tests that change on purpose: those that expect the category's name in the right card's title, and those that
  expect Forskel between Posteringer and Faste udgifter.

## 2. Full screen and the split

### 2.1 The detail stays in view (#88)

- In a split (full screen, and Alle posteringer), the detail column is sticky: it stays at the top of the viewport while
  the list scrolls, and scrolls on its own when it is taller than the viewport.
- When a row click opens or switches the detail in a split, the focus moves to the detail's heading without scrolling
  the page (`preventScroll`). On the normal page, where the detail replaces the list, the focus still moves as today.
- Closing the detail returns the focus to the row's button, also without scrolling the page in a split.
- A detail whose posting no longer matches the search stays open, without a marked row (as today, now a ruling).
- Tests: none new in happy-dom, which neither lays out nor scrolls; the existing focus tests stay. The Chrome check
  covers it (§6): in full screen and on Alle posteringer at 1440 px, scroll the list down, click a row, and the row
  stays where it was and the detail is in view.

### 2.2 The split has room (#89)

- The split's columns go from 3 : 2 to 11 : 9 (the detail 45 %).
- The detail's first box's label is "I perioden", without the date range: the range is in the top card.
  `da.detailInPeriod` becomes a plain text.
- In each box the label, the value and the count each stand on their own line.
- With the example data at 1440 and 1728 px, full screen and Alle posteringer, the boxes' texts each fit on one line
  (the Chrome check).
- Tests: the first box's text is "I perioden" with no date in it; the value and the count are separate elements, read
  by their text.
- Tests that change on purpose: those that expect the range in the first box.

### 2.3 The clicked posting shows in the detail (#87)

- When the marked row is among the newest 10, the detail shows the newest 10, as today.
- Otherwise it shows 10 rows around the marked one: the marked row, 4 newer and 5 older; with fewer than 5 older, more
  newer to make 10.
- "Vis alle N" still shows every row, and "Vis færre" goes back to the 10 around the marked row.
- Tests: a shop with 20 postings and the 12th newest marked shows the 8th to the 17th newest; with 15 postings and the
  13th newest marked it shows the 6th to the 15th; with the 3rd newest marked it shows the newest 10.

### 2.4 The detail's bars say what they show (#86)

- Each bar's item has a hover text (`title`) equal to its hidden label, "<unit>: <kr>".
- The unit in progress (started and not yet ended, as `isRunning` on the Søjler card) is drawn lighter, as on the Søjler
  card, and its hidden label and hover text end with " (i gang)" (`da.inProgress`).
- Tests: the running unit's list item's text contains `da.inProgress`; a finished unit's does not; each item's `title`
  equals its hidden label.

## 3. Search and filter

### 3.1 A hit on the bank's text says so (#90)

Today Posteringer gets the search text, but Alle posteringer does not pass it to its list, and a row's name button
holds only the shop.

- In Posteringer and Alle posteringer, while a search text is typed, a row whose shop does not contain the text but
  whose bank text does shows the bank text in grey, smaller, under the name button, in the same cell. It is not part
  of the button, so the button's accessible name stays the shop.
- A row matched on the shop, or with no shop (its name is the bank text), shows nothing extra.
- No highlighting of the matched part.
- Tests: with the search "abc", a row of another shop whose text holds "abc" shows that text in its cell, and its
  button's name is still the shop; a row whose shop holds "abc" does not show its text. Both on Posteringer and on
  Alle posteringer.

### 3.2 Amounts the Danish way (#91)

- The Beløb fields in "+ Filter" take Danish numbers: "1000", "1.000", "1000,50", "1.000,50", "0,5". A dot groups
  thousands (groups of three digits only); a comma starts one or two decimals. Spaces around the value are ignored.
- The fields are text fields with `inputmode="decimal"`.
- A field that is not empty and not understood is marked `aria-invalid="true"`, and a message under it, tied with
  `aria-describedby`, says how to write it: `da.amountInvalid`, "Skriv et beløb som 1.000,50". "Tilføj" then adds
  nothing and the menu stays open. An empty field has no message and means no bound, as today.
- An amount bound is held as a whole number of øre (`AmountRange` in øre), and `inAmount` compares øre with øre.
- The hash keeps the keys `min` and `max` in kroner. It reads digits, optionally a dot and one or two digits
  (`1000`, `1000.5`, `1000.50`); anything else does not parse and falls back as D20 says. It writes digits only for
  whole kroner and a dot with two digits otherwise (`max=1000.50`). Old links with whole kroner read as before. This
  is D47.
- The chip writes a whole bound without decimals and a bound with øre with two ("Beløb: 200–1.000,50 kr."); the wording is as today.
- Fra greater than Til is swapped, as today.
- Tests: each of the five forms above gives its bound; "1,5,0", "1.00", "1,505" and "abc" give the message and no
  chip; a posting of exactly 1.000,10 kr. is inside "Til 1.000,10"; `max=1000.50` and `max=1000` read from the hash,
  `max=1000.505` and `max=1,50` do not; a bound with øre round-trips through the hash.

### 3.3 A wrong date says so (#91)

- In the period picker, a typed date field that is not empty and is wrong is marked `aria-invalid="true"` and shows a
  message under it, tied with `aria-describedby`. The first that applies:
  1. not understood, or not a real date: `da.dateInvalid`, "Skriv en dato som 31.12.2026";
  2. after today: `da.dateFuture`, "Datoen er efter i dag";
  3. on Til only, before Fra: `da.dateOrder`, "Til er før Fra".
- The message shows when the field loses the focus or Enter is pressed in it, not while the user is still typing. It
  goes as soon as the field is right again.
- An empty field shows no message. "Vis" stays disabled while a field is wrong or empty, as today.
- Tests: "31.2.2026" in Fra, then blur, shows `da.dateInvalid` and marks the field; a date after today in Til shows
  `da.dateFuture`; a Til before Fra shows `da.dateOrder`; a valid pair shows no message.

### 3.4 The "+ Filter" menu can be searched (#92)

- A search field at the top of the menu, with the accessible name `da.filterSearch` ("Søg i filtre"), takes the focus
  when the menu opens. Its text is cleared when the menu closes.
- Typing narrows the main groups, the categories and the object groups to the names that contain the text,
  case-insensitive, as the postings search. A section with no hit is hidden; with no hit in any of them the menu says
  `da.noFilterHits` ("Ingen filtre passer").
- The order of the sections is Beløb, the object groups, Hovedgrupper, Kategorier. Beløb is not narrowed by the search.
- Tests: typing a category's name leaves that category and hides the others; text with no hit shows
  `da.noFilterHits`; the sections' order by role and name; the search field has the focus after opening.

## 5. Small points

### 5.1 The context line names a group once (#77)

On a subscription's detail, when the main group and the object group have the same name, the grey line shows the name
once. Test: a subscription whose object group shares its main group's name shows the name once in the context line.

### 5.2 Follow-ups with no visible change (#78, #50)

- A model test for a shop whose rows are on a ticked and an unticked account.
- `onDetailOpen` checks that the key resolves before it pushes a history entry.
- `detailResolves` uses the shared `within` instead of its own period check.
- `server/test/data.test.ts` compares `subscription.id` with the answer's subscription ids.
- `web/test/actions.test.ts`: the subscriptions-card trail case starts on that card, the trail case starts deeper than
  Alt forbrug, and `removeMainGroup` keeping the card is tested.
- The `<title>` of `docs/mockups/v3/abonnementer.html` and `docs/mockups/v4/budget.html` drops "tre udgaver".

### 5.3 Thousands on the axes (#98)

The bars' axis writes amounts in whole kroner with thousands dots ("10.000", "1.500", "-2.000"), not "10k". Test:
`axisKr` gives those texts. Tests that change on purpose: those expecting "k".

### 5.4 "Steget" without decimals (#98)

"Steget · 400 → 450 kr." uses whole kroner, as the Beløb column. When the two amounts would read the same in whole
kroner (149,70 → 150,20), both keep two decimals, as today. Tests: 400,00 → 450,50 gives "400 → 451"; 149,70 → 150,20
gives "149,70 → 150,20".

### 5.5 No "næste" where the status gives the date (#98)

In Faste udgifter, when a fixed expense's status is "Ikke set · ventet <date>" (`notSeen`), the "næste <date>" text is
not shown: it repeats a date that has passed. With any other status it shows as today. Test: a fixed expense not seen
shows no `da.nextOn` text; one expected in the future does.

### 5.6 A small change shows a decimal (#98)

A comparison whose change rounds to 0 % but is not zero shows one decimal with its sign ("-0,3 %"), so it agrees with
the arrow. A change smaller than 0,05 % shows `da.tinyChange` with its sign ("-under 0,1 %"). A change of exactly zero
shows "0 %" as today. Tests: each of the three through the key numbers' text.

### 5.7 The axis below zero (#98)

When a bar is below zero, the bars' axis goes no further below zero than that bar plus a margin of 10 % of the axis's
span, instead of to the next round step. This is a Chrome-check item; happy-dom draws no chart.

### 5.8 The sort arrow matches the signs (#98)

In Posteringer and Alle posteringer, the Beløb header shows the amount sort by the shown, signed numbers: the order with
the largest spending first ("-950,00" before "-12,50") shows ▲ and `aria-sort="ascending"`, its reverse ▼ and
`descending`. Only the header's display changes: the state, the sort keys `largest` and `smallest`, the hash and the
order on the first click stay as they are (D20, D40). Other columns and tables do not change. Tests: after the first
click on Beløb the header has `aria-sort="ascending"` and the first row is the largest spending; after a second,
`descending`. Tests that change on purpose: those expecting ▼ or `descending` on the first click.

### 5.9 "app" in the add-on's docs (#98)

`addon/DOCS.md` and `addon/README.md` say "app" where they mean what Home Assistant now calls an app (Settings → Apps).
The repo's own docs and the code keep "add-on". This is D48.
