# Til overs – design

**Real Home Assistant:** yes. Three new options (§5) are set on the real add-on after the release, through the house
session ([`releasing.md`](../../releasing.md#after-a-release)). Until they are set, nothing on the page changes.

**Issue:** closes #173. One branch, one PR.

**Approved:** by Thomas, 2026-11-04.

Thomas and Claude brainstormed this on 2026-11-04, one question at a time, with three mockups in the chat (not kept).
Thomas chose the table of months, reached from a key-number card on the overview. His words are quoted or named as his.
Every other detail is marked "Claude's call". Everything else in the [UI spec](2026-10-30-ui-ux-design.md) and the
later specs holds, the [top filter spec](2026-11-04-top-filter.md) and the
[fixed expenses over time spec](2026-11-04-fixed-expenses-over-time.md) above all. The names and amounts in this spec
are made up.

Later the same day, a review of the UX with Thomas changed the table. "Tilbage" means money moved back to a member's
private account, as Thomas meant it in the issue. It is its own column early in the table. The balance column is now
"Ændring på konti". A draw on the credit line is not in Til overs: Thomas, "vi gerne vil se det som et overforbrug det
måned". Every amount shows its sign, so a row adds up from left to right, and an "I alt" row closes the table.

## 1. Why

A household moves money from its members' own accounts into shared accounts, and tops them up when they run low.
Thomas: "lige nu har jeg ikke det store overblik og når kontoen er ved at være tom overføre vi extra". The page shows
what was spent, but not whether a month went in plus, or was saved by an extra deposit or a draw on a credit line.

- **Goal:** per month, the money the members put in, what went back to them, what was spent, what was left over, what
  the credit line did, what went to savings, and how much the accounts changed.
- **Goal:** a row reads as a sum from left to right, and the words do not collide.
- **Goal:** a draw on the credit line shows as one, not as a month that came out even. A month that needed a draw
  shows a Til overs below zero. Thomas: paying into the credit
  line "er stadig vigtig, da hvis vi inbetaler til kreditten bliver lånet mindere", and "i dag er det en skjult
  overførsel".
- **Goal:** money to savings shows as savings, apart from spending. Thomas, of a yearly payment to the children's
  savings: "kan vi lave det som overført til opsparing i stedet for?" The add-on does not move a posting: a savings
  category must be in an excluded main group, so the overview leaves it out of spending already (§3.1, §5).
- **Non-goal:** a forecast of the rest of the month. A second mockup showed one; Thomas chose the table.
- **Non-goal:** a chart of the year. The third mockup; not chosen.
- **Non-goal:** marking a deposit as "extra", which the issue asked for. It needs a member's regular amount, which the
  add-on does not know. Claude's call, told to Thomas in the review; the per-member line (§3.3) shows a top-up as a
  larger number. It can come later with an option for each member's regular amount.
- **Non-goal:** spreading a yearly payment over the months. Thomas: "hvad hvis vi starter med at det bare teller som
  et forbrug" (then as savings, above).
- **Non-goal:** a credit line held as a Firefly III liability, and savings held in asset accounts. A liability named
  in `credit_accounts` is ignored with a warning; a savings asset account counts as a daily account (§3.1). Claude's
  call: the household this is for holds its credit line as an asset account and its savings outside Firefly III.
- **Anti-goal:** a posting counted in two columns, or an Ændring på konti that does not add up. §3.2's buckets are
  exclusive and §4.2's sum rule is tested.

## 2. The way in and out

### 2.1 The key number

- The overview gets a key-number card **Til overs**, right after the period's own cards and before the object groups'
  cards (D53's row and widths), so it does not wrap alone onto a second row. Claude's call. Its number is the sum of
  the months' Til overs, rounded as §4.2 says, over the shown span: the chosen unit when there is one, else the trail's
  period. It shows no average per unit. As D53 says for every key number, it has no colour and no "+": a number below
  zero has its "−" only, in the normal text colour. Claude's call; the table's colours and signs (§4.2) are the
  table's own.
- **Its label** follows the other cards: "Til overs i perioden" with no unit chosen, "Til overs i <unit>" with one,
  the unit named as on the other cards (D70). Claude's call.
- **Under it,** in small text: "ind <Ind>", and ", lånt <amount>" when the span's Kreditten is a draw (§3.6). Both
  sum the same rounded months as the number (§4.2).
- **Its link** is **"Vis måneder →"**, worded as "Vis faste udgifter →". Thomas chose the card and its link ("ja lad os
  prøve det"); the wording "Vis" is Claude's call. Like every key-number card it is not a button (D56).
- **When it shows:** only when `SpendingData.leftOver` is true (§6.1), and only with no main group, no category and
  all accounts in the top filter. Til overs is about the household's accounts as a whole; a filter that narrows the
  spending would make Ind and Forbrug disagree. Claude's call.
- **Loading and errors:** while its answer loads, the card is a placeholder like the other key-number boxes. On an
  error the card is left out; the rest of the overview is not touched. Claude's call.

### 2.2 The view

- The link opens **the Til overs view**, `view=leftover` in the hash (§6.3), and pushes a history entry.
- **Out:** the ← button before the trail and Escape leave the view, exactly as the fixed-expenses view's way out (that
  spec §2), named "Tilbage til overblikket". Claude's call.
- **A month's name** is a button in the row's first cell; the row itself stays a table row, so the table keeps its
  roles. It opens the overview for that whole calendar month, pushing an entry: the preset Denne måned for the current
  month, else a custom period from the month's first to its last day. Thomas saw this in the mockup ("Et klik på en
  måned åbner oversigten for den måned"); the button in the first cell and the hash it writes are Claude's call.

## 3. The numbers

All amounts are in øre on the wire and in kr. on the page, as elsewhere. Each is computed per calendar month. A month
cut by the period's start or end counts only its days in the period. Postings after today count, as on the overview.

### 3.1 The accounts

- The **household's accounts** are every asset account.
- The **credit lines** are the asset accounts named in `credit_accounts`. A name that is a liability, or no account,
  is ignored (§5).
- The **daily accounts** are the other asset accounts, savings accounts too.

### 3.2 What is read, and the buckets

Only splits of type `withdrawal`, `deposit` and `transfer` are read. `opening balance` and `reconciliation` are
skipped by type. Every withdrawal from and deposit to a household account goes into exactly one bucket, the first that
takes it:

1. **Forbrug:** the posting is in the overview's spending, as `toPostings` makes it (spending or refund; D12, D15).
2. **Ind:** a deposit whose source is a revenue account named in `members`.
3. **Tilbage:** a withdrawal whose destination is an expense account named in `members`: money back to the member's
   private account. A withdrawal reaches this bucket only when its category is in an excluded main group: with no
   category, or a non-excluded one, `toPostings` counts it as spending, so it is Forbrug and the warning below fires.
4. **Opsparing:** a withdrawal or deposit in a category named in `savings_categories`.
5. **Øvrigt:** everything else.

A transfer between two household accounts is in no bucket: it only moves money between them (§3.6, §3.8). A transfer
whose other side is not a household account, such as a liability, counts on the household side like a withdrawal or a
deposit, and lands in Øvrigt, as `toPostings` never counts a transfer as spending. The server logs a warning
once per process (§5) when a split lands in Forbrug that would otherwise have been Ind, Tilbage or Opsparing, because
its category is missing or not in an excluded main group: the setup is wrong, and the column shows too little. Claude's
call.

### 3.3 Ind and Tilbage

**Ind** is the sum of bucket 2: the members' deposits into the household's accounts. Thomas: money from a member's
savings account into the shared account "er inbetaling til fælles konto". **Tilbage** is the sum of bucket 3: the money
moved back to the members' private accounts. Thomas: "det var tiltængt som penge der overføres tilbage til" a member's
private account, and it stands early in the table, right after Ind. Under each cell's amount is one muted line with
each member that has an amount in that bucket: "<name> <amount>", by name, joined by " · ". Firefly III's revenue
and expense accounts tell a member's money, not its category: a household can file it under more than one category.

### 3.4 Forbrug and Til overs

Forbrug is the sum of bucket 1: the same number the overview shows as spending for the same days, over all accounts.
**Til overs is Ind minus Tilbage minus Forbrug**, and can be below zero. Neither the credit line nor savings is in it:
a month that needed a draw on the credit line shows a Til overs below zero. Thomas, of a draw: "vi gerne vil se det som
et overforbrug det måned".

### 3.5 Opsparing

The withdrawals in bucket 4 minus its deposits. Below zero when more came back from savings than went to it in the
month: a household that borrows from its own savings and pays it back in the next month sees the two months cancel
out. Claude's call.

### 3.6 Kreditten

The change in the credit lines' summed balance from the read splits: every deposit into a credit line, minus every
withdrawal from it, plus every transfer into it from an account that is not a credit line, minus every transfer out of
it to such an account. Above zero the line was paid down; below zero it was drawn on. Claude's call: one net number,
not two, so no posting is counted twice. Thomas wanted the credit line early in the table, beside Tilbage; it stands
right after Til overs, the earliest place where the row still adds up (§4.2). Claude's call.

### 3.7 Øvrigt

The deposits in bucket 5 minus its withdrawals: interest, other income, money to another account of the household
outside Firefly III, and so on. It is summed from its own postings, not computed as a rest.

### 3.8 Ændring på konti

The change in the daily accounts' summed balance from the read splits, computed as in §3.6. A transfer between two
daily accounts adds nothing. Opening balances and reconciliations are left out, so in a month with one, Ændring på
konti differs from the bank by that amount. The name is Claude's, so that it does not collide with Tilbage or with
"Tilbage til overblikket".

## 4. The view

### 4.1 The trail

`← Alt forbrug › Til overs › <period> ▾`. Alt forbrug and Til overs are plain text; the period step is the `h1`, as top
filter spec §2.1 says for the period. It works as on the overview and pushes an entry. There is no unit step, no dashed
"Hovedgruppe" and no Konti: the view always covers every month of the period over all accounts. Claude's call.

### 4.2 The table and the sum rule

Under the trail, one table, newest month first, closed by an "I alt" row. The columns are those of the mockup Thomas
chose, Tilbage moved next to Ind as Thomas asked in the review, Kreditten right after Til overs (§3.6, Claude's
call), and Øvrigt added:

| Måned | Ind | Tilbage | Forbrug | Til overs | Kreditten | Opsparing | Øvrigt | Ændring på konti |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| okt. (i gang) | +31.000<br>Anna 18.000 · Bo 13.000 | −2.000<br>Anna 2.000 | −27.500 | +1.500 | 0 | 0 | 0 | +1.500 |
| sep. | +25.000<br>Anna 12.500 · Bo 12.500 | 0 | −23.800 | +1.200 | +10.000 lånt | −12.000 | +200 | −600 |
| aug. | +25.000<br>Anna 12.500 · Bo 12.500 | 0 | −30.000 | −5.000 | +6.000 lånt | 0 | 0 | +1.000 |
| I alt | +81.000<br>Anna 43.000 · Bo 38.000 | −2.000<br>Anna 2.000 | −81.300 | −2.300 | +16.000 lånt | −12.000 | +200 | +1.900 |

**Signs.** Every amount shows what it does to the daily accounts: "+" for money onto them, "−" for money off them, and
"0" without a sign. Ind is "+"; Tilbage and Forbrug are "−" (Forbrug is "+" in a month whose refunds are larger than its
spending). Opsparing is "−" when money went to savings. Kreditten is "−… betalt af" when the line was paid down and
"+… lånt" when it was drawn on, so its sign is the opposite of §3.6's number. The member lines under Ind and Tilbage
have no sign. Claude's call: a row then adds up from left to right.

**The sum rule.** For every month, and for "I alt", with the amounts as shown:

- **Til overs = Ind + Tilbage + Forbrug**
- **Ændring på konti = Til overs + Kreditten + Opsparing + Øvrigt**

The second holds because every split on a household account is in one bucket or is a transfer between two household
accounts. Each side is summed from its own postings, so the server's test of the rule, in øre, can fail.

**Rounding.** The page shows whole kroner, as `kr()` does. It rounds each month's parts once, to whole kroner the way
`kr()` rounds: each member's amounts, Kreditten, Opsparing, Øvrigt and Forbrug. Ind and Tilbage are the sums of their
rounded member amounts. Til overs and Ændring på konti are then computed from the rounded parts by the two rules
above, and "I alt" sums the rounded month cells. So every row and "I alt" add up exactly as shown, and the card, which
sums the same rounded months, matches "I alt". A shown sum can differ by a few kroner from the same amount rounded
once: "I alt" Forbrug from the overview's "Forbrug i perioden", and Ændring på konti from the
true change. Claude's call, after the builder check.

- **"I alt"** sums each column over the listed months, with the member lines summed per member. Its Til overs equals
  the card's number when no unit is chosen. It is not a button. Claude's call.
- **Hidden columns:** Kreditten is left out when `hasCredit` is false, and Opsparing when `hasSavings` is false
  (§6.2). Claude's call: a column of zeros the household never set up says nothing.
- **Colours:** Til overs is in the success colour above zero and the danger colour below. A Kreditten draw ("lånt") is
  in the danger colour, as the mockup showed. Ændring på konti below zero is in the danger colour. Claude's call.
- The current month's name is followed by "(i gang)". Claude's call.
- A month is named without its year when every month of the period lies in one calendar year, else "okt. 2025" (D70).
- A period of more than 24 months still lists months, and the view scrolls. Claude's call: the household looks at
  months.
- A month with no postings still gets a row of zeros, so a gap is visible. Claude's call.

### 4.3 States

While the answer loads, the table shows a placeholder row per month of the period. On an error, the page's error card
(D18). With `leftOver` false and `view=leftover` in the hash (a stale link), the page shows the overview and replaces
the entry without the view. Claude's call.

## 5. The options

Three new options in `addon/config.yaml`, labelled in `addon/translations/en.yaml` and `da.yaml`:

| Option | Type | Default | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `members` | list of str | `[]` | The household's members: the revenue accounts of their money in and the expense accounts of the same names for their money back |
| `savings_categories` | list of str | `[]` | The categories of money to or from savings |
| `credit_accounts` | list of str | `[]` | The asset accounts that are credit lines |

- Members are matched by a deposit's `source_name` with `source_type` "Revenue account", and by a withdrawal's
  `destination_name` with `destination_type` "Expense account". Credit lines are matched by an asset account's name,
  savings by `category_name`.
- The span-free read (D49) also reads `accounts?type=revenue` and `accounts?type=liabilities`. At the first span-free
  read of the process, the server logs once each member name that matches no revenue account (a member's expense
  account is neither read nor checked), each other name that matches nothing, and each credit line that is a liability.
- `leftOver` (§6.1) is true when at least one member name matches a revenue account. With none, the card and the view
  do not show.
- `savings_categories` and `credit_accounts` may be empty: then, or when no name in them matches, the Opsparing or
  Kreditten column is left out (§4.2), and the money shows in Øvrigt or Ændring på konti.
- `addon/DOCS.md` explains the options with a made-up example, and says that the categories of the members' deposits,
  of the money back to them and of savings belong in an excluded main group (§3.2).

## 6. The server, the answer and the hash

### 6.1 `/api/data`

`SpendingData` gets `leftOver: boolean` (§5). Nothing else in it changes.

### 6.2 `/api/left-over`

`GET /api/left-over?start=YYYY-MM-DD&end=YYYY-MM-DD` answers a `LeftOverData` in `addon/shared/`:

- `range` and `today`, as in `SpendingData`;
- `hasSavings`: true when at least one name in `savings_categories` matches a category; `hasCredit`: true when at
  least one name in `credit_accounts` matches an asset account (§3.1). They drive §4.2's hidden columns;
- `months`, newest first, each a `LeftOverMonth` with `month` (`YYYY-MM`) and these amounts in øre, each with §3's
  sign, not the sign the page shows:
  - `paidIn` and `back`: Ind and Tilbage (§3.3), zero or above;
  - `members`: `{ name, paidIn, back }[]` by name, only the members with an amount that is not zero;
  - `spending`: Forbrug (§3.4);
  - `savings`: Opsparing (§3.5), above zero when money went to savings;
  - `credit`: Kreditten (§3.6), above zero when the line was paid down;
  - `other`: Øvrigt (§3.7);
  - `change`: Ændring på konti (§3.8).

So `change = paidIn − back − spending − credit − savings + other`. Til overs, Ændring på konti as shown, the shown
signs and the "I alt" row are computed by the page from the rounded parts (§4.2); the server's `change` is for the test
of the sum rule.

Errors are `DataError`'s codes (D18). The server reads the period's transactions once with `type=all` and keeps the
raw groups in the cache per span (D49), so `/api/data` and `/api/left-over` for the same span share one read.
The page asks `/api/left-over` for the period itself. For a one-unit period `/api/data` reads 12 units before it
(D53), so the two spans differ and the read is not shared; that costs time, not correctness. Claude's call.

### 6.3 The hash

- `view=leftover` joins `view=full` (D60) and `view=fixed` (D66).
- On open, `unit` is removed from the hash; a way out that replaces the entry comes back without it. Reading
  `view=leftover` drops any `group`, `category` and `accounts` it finds (D20's fallback). `card`, `detail`, `q`,
  `shop`, `about`, `under`, `over`, `min` and `max` stay untouched for the way back, as in the fixed-expenses view.
- A row's month is the whole calendar month, also when the period cuts it, so the overview it opens can show more
  than the row. Claude's call.
