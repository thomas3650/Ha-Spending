# Til overs – design

**Real Home Assistant:** yes. Three new options (§5) are set on the real add-on after the release, through the house
session ([`releasing.md`](../../releasing.md#after-a-release)). Until they are set, nothing on the page changes.

**Issue:** closes #173. One branch, one PR.

**Approved:** not yet.

Thomas and Claude brainstormed this on 2026-11-04, one question at a time, with three mockups in the chat (not kept).
Thomas chose the table of months, reached from a key-number card on the overview. His words are quoted or named as his.
Every other detail is marked "Claude's call". Everything else in the [UI spec](2026-10-30-ui-ux-design.md) and the
later specs holds, the [top filter spec](2026-11-04-top-filter.md) and the
[fixed expenses over time spec](2026-11-04-fixed-expenses-over-time.md) above all. The names and amounts in this spec
are made up.

## 1. Why

A household moves money from its members' own accounts into shared accounts, and tops them up when they run low.
Thomas: "lige nu har jeg ikke det store overblik og når kontoen er ved at være tom overføre vi extra". The page shows
what was spent, but not whether a month went in plus, or was saved by an extra deposit or a draw on a credit line.

- **Goal:** per month, the money the members put in, what was spent, what was left over, what went to savings, what the
  credit line did, and what stayed on the accounts.
- **Goal:** a draw on the credit line shows as one, not as a month that came out even. Thomas: paying into the credit
  line "er stadig vigtig, da hvis vi inbetaler til kreditten bliver lånet mindere", and "i dag er det en skjult
  overførsel".
- **Goal:** money to savings is not spending. Thomas, of the yearly payment to the children's savings: "kan vi lave det
  som overført til opsparing i stedet for?"
- **Non-goal:** a forecast of the rest of the month. A second mockup showed one; Thomas chose the table.
- **Non-goal:** a chart of the year. The third mockup; not chosen.
- **Non-goal:** marking a deposit as "extra". It needs a member's regular amount, which the add-on does not know.
  Claude's call; the per-member split in §3.2 shows a top-up as a larger number.
- **Non-goal:** spreading a yearly payment over the months. Thomas: "hvad hvis vi starter med at det bare teller som
  et forbrug" (then as savings, above).
- **Anti-goal:** a Tilbage that does not match the change in the accounts' balance. §4.2 makes them the same by
  definition.

## 2. The way in and out

- **The key number.** The overview gets a key-number card **"Til overs"** after the others. Its number is §3's Til
  overs for the trail's period and chosen unit. Under it, in small text: "ind <Ind> · lånt <amount>", the last part only
  when the credit line was drawn on (§3.5 negative). Its link is **"Se måneder →"**. Thomas chose the card and its link
  ("ja lad os prøve det"). Like every key-number card, it is not a button (D56).
- **When it shows.** Only when the `members` option (§5) is not empty, and only at the top level with no main group,
  no category and all accounts in the top filter. Til overs is about the household's accounts as a whole, so a filter
  that narrows the spending would make Ind and Forbrug disagree. Claude's call.
- **The link** opens **the Til overs view**, `view=leftover` in the hash (§6), and pushes a history entry. The trail's
  period comes along. The chosen unit does not: the view lists every month of the period.
- **Out:** the ← button before the trail and Escape leave the view, exactly as the fixed-expenses view's way out (that
  spec §2), named "Tilbage til overblikket". Claude's call.
- **A month's row** is a button: it opens the overview with the period set to that month, pushing an entry. Thomas saw
  this in the mockup ("Et klik på en måned åbner oversigten for den måned").

## 3. The numbers

All amounts are in øre on the wire and in kr. on the page, as elsewhere. Each is computed per calendar month. A month
cut by the period's start or end counts only its days in the period. "Today" is the answer's `today`, and the current
month counts up to today.

The **household's accounts** are every asset account. The **credit lines** are the asset accounts named in the
`credit_accounts` option. The **daily accounts** are the other asset accounts.

### 3.1 Forbrug

The same number the overview shows as spending for the month, over all accounts: spending minus refunds, with the
excluded main groups left out (D12, D15). Nothing new is counted. A test holds that the view's Forbrug for a month
equals the overview's for that month.

### 3.2 Ind

The sum of the deposits whose source is a revenue account named in the `members` option, into any household account,
credit lines included, whatever their category. Under the sum, one line per member with an amount above zero: "<name>
<amount>", by name. Firefly III's revenue account is what tells a member's deposit, not its category: a household can
file its members' deposits under more than one category.

### 3.3 Til overs

Ind minus Forbrug. It can be below zero.

### 3.4 Opsparing

The withdrawals in a category named in the `savings_categories` option, minus the deposits in such a category, on any
household account. Below zero when more came back from savings than went to it in the month: a household that borrows
from its own savings and pays it back in the next month sees the two months cancel out. Claude's call. A transfer has no
category and never counts here.

### 3.5 Kreditten

The change in the credit lines' summed balance over the month: every deposit into a credit line, minus every
withdrawal from it, plus every transfer into it, minus every transfer out of it. Opening balances and reconciliations
are left out. Above zero, the line was paid down: the row reads "betalt af <amount>". Below zero, it was drawn on:
"lånt <amount>", in the danger colour, as the mockup showed. Zero reads "0". Claude's call: one net number per month,
not two, so that §4.2 holds with no double counting.

### 3.6 Øvrigt

Every other change in the household's balance that §3.2 to §3.5 do not explain: deposits from anyone not in `members`
in an excluded main group (interest, other income), withdrawals in an excluded main group that are not savings, and so
on. It is computed as the rest (§4.2), so it is always right. Claude's call: without it Tilbage would not match the
bank.

### 3.7 Tilbage

The change in the daily accounts' summed balance over the month, computed as in §3.5. A transfer between two daily
accounts adds nothing.

## 4. The view

### 4.1 The table

Under the trail, one table, newest month first. The columns, as in the mockup Thomas chose:

| Måned | Ind | Forbrug | Til overs | Opsparing | Kreditten | Øvrigt | Tilbage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| okt. | 31.000 (Anna 18.000 · Bo 13.000) | 27.500 | 3.500 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 3.500 |
| sep. | 25.000 | 23.800 | 1.200 | 12.000 | lånt 10.000 | 0 | −800 |

- Til overs is in the success colour above zero and the danger colour below. Tilbage below zero is in the danger
  colour. Claude's call.
- The current month's row is marked "til <day>." after its name, for example "nov., til 4.". Claude's call.
- A month name follows D70's rule: no year within one year, else "okt. 2025".
- A period of more than 24 months still lists months; it is long, and the view scrolls. Claude's call: the household
  looks at months.
- Empty months (no postings at all) still get a row of zeros, so a gap is visible. Claude's call.

### 4.2 The sum rule

For every month: **Tilbage = Til overs − Opsparing − Kreditten + Øvrigt**. Øvrigt is defined by this rule (§3.6), so
it holds by construction, and the server's test checks it on the fake data with each kind of posting.

### 4.3 States

While the answer loads, the table shows a placeholder of the period's months. On an error, the page's usual error card
(D18). With `members` empty and `view=leftover` in the hash, the page shows the overview and drops the view (a stale
link). Claude's call.

## 5. The options

Three new options in `addon/config.yaml`, with labels in `addon/translations/en.yaml` and `da.yaml`:

| Option | Type | Default | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `members` | list of str | `[]` | The revenue accounts whose deposits are the household's members' money in |
| `savings_categories` | list of str | `[]` | The categories whose postings are money to or from savings |
| `credit_accounts` | list of str | `[]` | The asset accounts that are credit lines |

A name that does not exist in Firefly III is ignored, and the server logs it once at start. With `members` empty the
card and the view do not show (§2). The other two may be empty: then Opsparing or Kreditten is zero every month, and
the money shows in Øvrigt or Tilbage. Claude's call.

`addon/DOCS.md` explains the three options with a made-up example.

## 6. The server and the hash

- **The server** answers `GET /api/left-over?start=YYYY-MM-DD&end=YYYY-MM-DD` with the months of §3, typed in
  `addon/shared/`, with the same errors as `/api/data` (D18) and the same cache rule (D49). It reads the transactions of
  the period with `type=all`, as `/api/data` does, and computes the months from them. A separate route keeps
  `/api/data` as it is. Claude's call.
- **The overview's key number** reads the same route for the trail's period, only when the card shows (§2).
- **The hash** gets `view=leftover`, beside `view=full` (D60) and `view=fixed` (D66). The trail's period is kept as
  usual.

## 7. Tests

As the [testing doc](../../testing.md) asks, through public interfaces and made-up data only:

- The server: each number of §3 for a month with each kind of posting (a member's deposit to a daily account and to a
  credit line, spending, a refund, a savings withdrawal and its return, a transfer between daily accounts, a transfer
  to and from a credit line, interest, other income, an opening balance); the sum rule of §4.2; a month cut by the
  period; an unknown name in an option.
- The page: the card shows and hides as §2 says; the link opens the view; ← and Escape leave it; a row opens the
  overview for its month; the colours and texts of §3.5 and §4.1; the stale link of §4.3.
- The fake Firefly III gets two members, a savings category and a credit line in its example data.
- The Chrome check (D45) on the view and the card, at full screen.

## 8. Decisions

The build adds D-entries for: the card and its rule for showing (§2), the view in the hash (§6), the numbers and the
sum rule (§3, §4.2), and the three options (§5).
