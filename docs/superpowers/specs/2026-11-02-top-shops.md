# The shops with the most spending – design

**Real Home Assistant:** no change there from this work. The release reaches the house through the house session
([`releasing.md`](../../releasing.md#after-a-release)). No new option. The add-on starts to keep one file of its own in
`/data`, the list of hidden shops (§4); it needs no setup.

**Issue:** #55.

Thomas wants to find out at which shops he spends the most: across all spending, and within a main group or a category
such as Dagligvarer, where Føtex, Netto and Rema compete. Since #53 every posting can carry its shop
([shop search spec](2026-11-01-shop-search.md) §1). On 2026-11-02 he chose, from screenshots shown in chat, a fourth
choice on the right card (variant A, §1). Rent and the municipality top the list at Alt forbrug, and he does not want
them there; some other fixed expenses he does want to see. So he chose to hide shops one by one (§3) over leaving out
every posting linked to a bill.

This spec adds to the [UI spec](2026-10-30-ui-ux-design.md) §4 and to the
[technical spec](2026-10-30-technical-design.md) §1, §5 and §6 where it says so. Everything else there holds.

**Claude's calls, for Thomas to see in the PR:**

  (§1).
- It does not follow the search or the chosen shop; the chosen shop's row is marked instead (§1).

  left out (§2).

  the name in the body, never anything in Firefly III. This is D43 (§4).

  shows every shop and has no "Skjul" (§3, §4).
- Until the hidden shops have answered, the card shows "Henter …", so a hidden shop never flashes at the top (§3).

## 1. The Butikker card

- **Where:** a fourth choice on the right card's switch, **Butikker**, after Faste udgifter (and after Forskel when a
  unit is chosen). The hash value is `card=shops` ([D20](../../decisions.md) lists the keys, not the values, so it
  needs no change).
- **When it is offered:** when at least one posting in the answer (`ctx.data.postings`, not only the shown ones) has a
  shop. Otherwise there is no Butikker button, and `card=shops` falls back to Søjler as an unknown card does.
- **The title:** "Butikker" at Alt forbrug, "Butikker i Mad" at a main group or a category (the level's name, as
  Posteringer's title has it). A chosen unit shows its chip beside the title, as on Søjler and Posteringer; the chip's
  ✕ clears the unit and keeps the card.
- **The base:** the shown postings at the level and the accounts (`ctx.shown`), within the chosen unit when one is
  chosen and otherwise the period: `within(ctx.shown, ctx.chosen ?? ctx.timeline.period)`, as the Posteringer card's
  base. The search and the chosen shop do not narrow it.
- **Full screen** ([D37](../../decisions.md)) works as for the other choices.

## 2. The list

- **A row per shop** in the base, its sum the net of its postings there (refunds subtracted), the rule of
  `shopSuggestions`. A shop whose net is zero or less is left out.
- **The order:** the biggest sum first; ties by name with `localeCompare(…, "da")`.
- **A row shows** its place (1, 2, 3 …), the shop's name, a bar, the sum in kroner, and its share in whole per cent of
  the base's total (`total(base)`, hidden shops and postings with no shop included), as "33 %". A share under 0.5 %
  reads "0 %". Every bar on the card, also a shown hidden row's (§3), is scaled against the biggest sum among the rows
  on the card at that moment, so no bar runs past full length.
- **The name is a button.** A click chooses that shop ([D35](../../decisions.md)), empties the search as choosing a
  suggestion does, and switches the card to Posteringer: one click, one history entry. The Posteringer card then shows
  the chip "Butik: Netto" and only its postings. The button's accessible name is the shop's name and sum, as a
  suggestion's ("Netto, 12.101 kr.").
- **The chosen shop:** when a shop is chosen, its row's button is `aria-pressed="true"` and marked.
- **"Skjul":** each row ends with a button "Skjul", named "Skjul Netto". It hides the shop (§3).
- **The first 10:** only the first 10 visible rows show. When there are more, a button under them reads "Vis alle 39"
  (the count of visible rows) and shows the rest; it then reads "Vis færre". Full screen shows every row and no
  button. Whether all are shown is held by the app, like the cards' sort ([D40](../../decisions.md)): not in the hash,
  and back to 10 on a reload. A change of level, period or accounts keeps it. The limit counts visible rows only;
  shown hidden rows (§3) are all shown.
- **Uden butik:** when the net of the base's postings with no shop is above zero, a line under the list reads "Uden
  butik: 1.557 kr.". It is not a row, has no place and no button.
- **Nothing to show,** in this order: a base with no posting gives `da.noSpending`, as the other choices do; a base
  with postings but no row and no hidden shop gives "Ingen butikker at vise". The unit chip stays to be cleared. With
  every shop hidden, the list is empty but "Vis skjulte" still shows (§3).

## 3. Hidden shops

- **Hiding:** "Skjul" sends the shop to the server (§4) and, once the server answers, removes the row. The other rows
  close up and keep their order; the places are counted again over the visible rows. Hiding adds no history entry: it
  is not a view choice. The page then takes the server's answer as the hidden list.
- **Everywhere:** a hidden shop is hidden on every level, period and account choice, and on every device that opens
  the page, since the server keeps the list.
- **"Vis skjulte":** when any of the base's shops is hidden, a button under the list (after "Vis alle", before "Uden
  butik") reads "Vis skjulte (2 · 129.133 kr.)": their count and their summed net in the base. A click shows them under
  the visible rows, in their own order by sum, grey, with no place number and a button "Vis" (named "Vis Udlejer")
  instead of "Skjul"; the button then reads "Skjul skjulte". Their names still choose the shop. Whether they are shown
  is held by the app as "Vis alle" is. A hidden shop that is not in the base is not counted.
- **Showing again:** "Vis" sends it to the server and, once it answers, moves the row back among the visible rows.
- **A failed write:** the row stays as it was and a line under the card's title reads "Kunne ikke gemme. Prøv igen."
  until the next successful write or a reload.
- **Before the read answers:** the page asks for the hidden shops when it starts, beside `/api/data` and not waiting
  for it (§4). Until that answer has come, the Butikker card shows "Henter …" in place of the list, so a hidden shop
  never flashes at the top. The other choices do not wait for it.
- **A failed read:** the list shows every shop, with no "Skjul", no "Vis skjulte" and a line "Skjulte butikker kunne
  ikke hentes." The rest of the page works as usual. The page asks again on its next start.
- **Only this list:** the circle, the bars, the key numbers, the Posteringer card, Alle posteringer and the shop
  suggestions do not change with a hidden shop.

## 4. The server keeps the hidden shops

This is the first route on port 8099 that writes, and the add-on's first file of its own. It changes the technical
spec §1 ("never writes") in that the add-on writes this file, §5 ("no route writes") in that the POST below writes,
and §6 (the page fetches `/api/data` "and nothing else on a click") in that the page also reads the hidden shops at
start and writes on a click. Nothing is ever written to Firefly III from port 8099.

- **The file:** `/data/hidden-shops.json`, or the path in `HA_SPENDING_HIDDEN_SHOPS` (read in
  `addon/server/src/env.ts`, which also holds the default). It holds `{"shops": ["Kommunen", "Udlejer"]}`, sorted with
  `localeCompare(…, "da")`. The server reads the file on every request and keeps nothing in memory, so a change on
  disk shows at once.
- **A missing or unreadable file** is no hidden shops. An unreadable one (not JSON, or not that shape) is logged as
  "hidden shops: unreadable, treated as empty" (no content); the next write replaces it. So a broken file never stops
  hiding from the page. Any other error reading or writing it (a permission or disk error) answers 500
  `internal` and changes nothing.
- **Writing:** the server writes a temporary file beside it and renames it into place, so a crash never leaves half a
  file. Writes are done one at a time, in the order they arrive, each on the file as the one before left it.
- **The fast loop** sets `HA_SPENDING_HIDDEN_SHOPS` to `$PWD/local/hidden-shops.json` in `scripts/develop`, as it does
  `HA_SPENDING_OPTIONS` (`local/` is git-ignored). Tests use a temporary directory.
- **`GET /api/hidden-shops`** answers `{"shops": [...]}` (typed `HiddenShops` in `addon/shared/`), with
  `Cache-Control: no-store`.
- **`POST /api/hidden-shops`** with the body `{"shop": "Udlejer", "hidden": true}` hides a shop; `"hidden": false`
  shows it again. The name is in the body, never in the URL, so no proxy rewrites or logs it. It is idempotent: hiding
  a hidden shop or showing a shown one changes nothing. It answers the whole list as the GET does, with the same
  header. One shop per request, so two devices cannot undo each other's change with a stale list.
- **The limits, in the code:**
  - `Content-Type`'s media type, its parameters such as `charset` set aside, must be `application/json`; otherwise
    415 `unsupported`. A cross-site request with that type is preflighted, and the server answers no CORS, so only
    the page itself can write. This matters most for the fast loop, which has no ingress check ([D13](../../decisions.md));
  - the body is at most 1 kB and is exactly an object with `shop` and `hidden`; `shop` is a string of 1 to 200
    characters with no control characters, compared and kept exactly as given (as [D35](../../decisions.md)), and
    `hidden` a boolean; otherwise 400 `bad-request`;
  - at most 500 hidden shops; hiding a new one past that answers 409 `full`;
  - a failed write answers 500 `internal`;
  - errors answer `{"error": "<code>"}`, the codes typed in `addon/shared/`. The log line is the method, the route
    pattern and the code (`POST /api/hidden-shops: full`), never the shop.
- **The ingress check** covers these routes as it covers the others ([D5](../../decisions.md)). A `PUT` or `DELETE`
  on the route, as any other unknown `/api/…`, is 404.
- **The cache** of `/api/data` is untouched; the hidden shops are not in that answer.

## 5. Tests

By role, accessible name and text, made-up data only (hard rule 5).

- **Server** (`addon/server/test/`): GET with no file is empty and has `Cache-Control: no-store`; hiding answers the
  sorted list, twice is the same; showing again works, and of a shop not hidden changes nothing; the file survives a
  new app on the same path, and a change written to the file by hand shows on the next GET; a name with `/`, `%`,
  `..`, `æøå` and spaces round-trips; two hides of different shops sent at once both end in the file; a wrong content
  type is 415; an empty or 201-character name, one with a control character, a missing or non-boolean `hidden`, an
  extra key, and a body over 1 kB are 400; the 501st is 409; an unreadable file reads as empty, is logged, and is
  replaced by the next write; a log line after an error has no shop name; a peer that is not the ingress address gets
  403.
- **The model:** the rows' sums are net, ordered by sum then name, zero and below left out; shares are of the whole
  base; "Uden butik" sums postings with no shop and is absent at zero or below; hidden shops are split out with their
  count and sum; the base follows a chosen unit and not the search or the chosen shop; the bars' scale is the biggest
  row on the card, also with hidden rows shown.
- **The page:**
  - Butikker is offered only when a posting has a shop, and `card=shops` falls back to Søjler without one;
  - the card's title at Alt forbrug, at a main group and at a category, and the rows follow the level, the accounts,
    the period and a chosen unit, with the unit chip;
  - 10 rows and "Vis alle N", then all and "Vis færre"; full screen shows all and no button;
  - a click on a name shows Posteringer with the shop's chip and its postings, and the search emptied; Back returns to
    Butikker;
  - the chosen shop's row is pressed;
  - "Henter …" until the hidden shops have answered, and no hidden shop shown before;
  - "Skjul" removes the row after the server answers, "Vis skjulte" shows it greyed with "Vis", and "Vis" brings it
    back; the shares and the circle's total do not change;
  - a failed write keeps the row and shows the message; a failed read shows every shop with no "Skjul" and the
    message;
  - "Uden butik" shows with its sum, and not without such postings;
  - `da.noSpending` with no posting, and "Ingen butikker at vise" with postings but no shop.

The Chrome check ([`testing.md`](../../testing.md)): the card at Alt forbrug, at a main group and with a chosen unit;
hiding and showing a shop, also after a reload; "Vis alle" and full screen. At 1440 px and at 420 px, where the
card's switch now holds up to five buttons and must wrap rather than scroll the page.
